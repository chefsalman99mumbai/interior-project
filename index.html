02 / Services

FROM
SHELL
TO SOUL.

FIZZA INTERIOR delivers the full built environment in five disciplined pillars — each one a specialist team, all coordinated under a single turnkey contract.

01

Turnkey Interior Solutions

End-to-end design and execution for premium residential and commercial spaces — one contract, one site team, one final handover.

Residential

Residential Interior Fit-Outs

Full home transformations, space planning and premium styling for apartments, villas and penthouses.

Commercial

Commercial & Corporate Workspaces

Full-scale office renovations, bare-shell fit-outs, conference rooms, executive cabins and retail outlets.

Joinery

Customized Furniture & Joinery

Bespoke wardrobe manufacturing, modular kitchen setups, storage units and loose furniture tailored to the space.

02

Civil & Finishing Works

Structural finishing and surface preparation executed to architectural tolerance — the invisible discipline behind every precise interior.

Exterior

External Plastering & Painting

High-durability external plastering, weather-resistant painting, texture coatings and full scaffolding execution.

Interior

Internal Plastering & Surface Preparation

Levelled internal wall plastering, smooth wall putty application and architectural internal painting.

Flooring

Premium Tile & Flooring Layouts

Precision installation of vitrified tiles, marble, granite, ceramic tiling and specialised wall cladding.

03

False Ceiling & Surface Art

Decorative and structural overhead works — where engineering meets ornament, and the ceiling becomes part of the design.

Gypsum

Gypsum False Ceilings & Partitions

Modern grid ceilings, drop ceilings and functional space partition walls for residential and commercial interiors.

POP

Plaster of Paris (POP) Artistry

Elegant cornices, traditional mouldings, custom ceiling domes and decorative wall finishes.

04

Acoustic & Soundproofing Engineering

A specialist niche for high-value spaces — studios, home theatres, auditoriums and boardrooms where silence is part of the spec.

Barrier

Architectural Soundproofing

High-performance sound barrier installations for walls, floors and ceilings.

Treatment

Acoustic Treatment & Insulation

Echo reduction, acoustic panelling, perforated ceiling tiles and fabric padding for home theatres, recording studios, auditoriums and conference rooms.

05

Home Automation & Smart Living

Intelligent systems engineered into the architecture — lighting, climate, security and entertainment working as one seamless, voice-controlled environment.

Lighting & Climate

Smart Lighting, Shading & HVAC

Scene-based lighting, motorised blinds and zoned climate control with app, voice and wall-panel command.

Security

Access Control & Surveillance

Smart locks, video door phones, CCTV and sensor networks with remote monitoring and automated alerts.

AV & Control

Multi-Room Audio & Centralised Hubs

Distributed audio, home theatre and single-app control across lighting, climate, security and curtains.

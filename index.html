Frequently Asked

QUESTIONS.

What does a turnkey interior contractor in Mumbai do?

A turnkey interior contractor handles design, civil work, MEP coordination, joinery, finishes, furniture and final styling under one contract. FIZZA INTERIOR delivers every trade from a single point of accountability, from shell to handover, across Mumbai.

Where is FIZZA INTERIOR located in Mumbai?

FIZZA INTERIOR is based at 810, Opposite City Mall, Next to Global Junction, Veera Desai Industrial Estates, New Link Road, Andheri-West, Mumbai 400053. We execute projects across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

How long does a turnkey residential fit-out take?

A typical 2BHK or 3BHK apartment fit-out takes 8 to 14 weeks depending on scope, site readiness and material lead times. Larger villas and penthouses run 16 to 24 weeks. FIZZA INTERIOR issues a fixed programme at contract stage.

Do you handle commercial and corporate interior projects?

Yes. FIZZA INTERIOR executes office fit-outs, bare-shell renovations, conference rooms, executive cabins and retail outlets across Mumbai, coordinating all trades under one turnkey contract.

What is acoustic soundproofing and do you provide it?

Acoustic soundproofing uses barrier and absorption materials to prevent noise transmission and reduce echo. FIZZA INTERIOR installs architectural soundproofing and acoustic treatment for home theatres, recording studios, auditoriums, boardrooms and premium residences.

Do you install home automation systems?

Yes. FIZZA INTERIOR installs smart lighting, motorised shading, zoned HVAC, access control, CCTV surveillance, multi-room audio and centralised smart home hubs with app, voice and wall-panel control.

Can you build custom furniture and modular kitchens?

Yes. FIZZA INTERIOR manufactures bespoke wardrobes, modular kitchens, TV units, storage systems and loose furniture in its own joinery setup, tailored to each site's dimensions and finish palette.

Do you undertake civil finishing work only, without full interiors?

Yes. Civil finishing and surface preparation can be contracted as a standalone scope. FIZZA INTERIOR frequently serves as the civil and finishes partner for other designers and developers.
